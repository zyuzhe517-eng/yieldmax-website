What our available SGS reports show

  • Tensile-strength result for the identified submitted galvanised-wire specimen
  • Coating-mass result for the identified submitted specimen
  • Coating-adhesion result under the stated test conditions
  • Report number, specimen diameter and result scope remain visible for review

Important scope note

The existing SGS reports relate to specific submitted galvanised-wire samples. They do not certify every production batch or the complete finished fence. Finished-fence height, spacing, roll dimensions and packing are factory checked. If you require order-specific third-party inspection, tell us before quotation so it can be defined and costed.
