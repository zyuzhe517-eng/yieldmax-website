Our Story

YIELD MAX was founded with a single focus: supplying high-quality, AS 4534 & AS 2423 compliant fixed knot fencing to the Australian agricultural market. Over more than 10 years, we have built lasting relationships with importers, distributors, and rural supply businesses across Australia.

Our manufacturing expertise, rigorous quality control, and deep understanding of Australian and New Zealand standards set us apart. Every roll of fence we ship reflects our commitment to consistency — what you approve in the sample is what arrives on site.

Today, we are actively expanding into the New Zealand market, bringing the same proven quality and service to Kiwi farmers and fencing contractors.

Standards & Certifications

Built to Australian & NZ Standards

Coating Standard

AS/NZS 4534 — Coatings on Steel Wire

All wire used in our products is coated to AS/NZS 4534 — the Australian/New Zealand standard for zinc (Z) and zinc/aluminium-alloy (ZA) coatings on steel wire. Class and coating mass are matched to the specified environment.

Product Standard

AS 2423 — Coated Wire Fencing Products

Our finished fixed knot fence is built to AS 2423 — the standard covering coated steel wire fencing products for terrestrial, aquatic and general use. Mesh geometry, joint integrity and roll specs are verified per AS 2423.

Coating Option · Z

Hot-Dip Galvanized (HDG)

Pure zinc coating to AS/NZS 4534 — Class A or Class B coating mass. Cost-effective, proven choice for standard inland rural environments across Australia.

Coating Option · ZA

Zinc/Aluminium Alloy (Zn-Al)

Zinc/10%-aluminium alloy coating to AS/NZS 4534. 2–3× longer service life than HDG in coastal, humid or chemically aggressive zones. Recommended for NZ and coastal AU.
