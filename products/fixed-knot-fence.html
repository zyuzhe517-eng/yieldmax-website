YIELD MAX manufactures fixed knot fence to the buyer's agreed specification. We work with importers, distributors and agricultural supply businesses on bulk and full-container orders.

Continuous Stays. Separate Knot Wire.

Horizontal line wires and continuous vertical stay wires are secured together with a separate knot wire to form the fence mesh.

Wire diameter, tensile requirements, coating and mesh layout are reviewed together for each configuration. We agree the finished-fence specification with the buyer before production.

Wire & finish

Agree the Material Specification Before Production

Line wire, stay wire and knot wire serve different roles. The technical sheet sets out the proposed wire diameters, strength requirements and coating for the selected configuration.

Hot-dip galvanised wire and alloy-coated options can be discussed against the buyer's coating specification and intended service conditions. The coating type, class and applicable standard are confirmed for the order.

Documentation for Your Review

Detailed technical data and available test documentation can be shared directly following your enquiry. Any test report is reviewed with its identified sample, diameter and test scope. Order-specific inspection requirements are agreed before production.
