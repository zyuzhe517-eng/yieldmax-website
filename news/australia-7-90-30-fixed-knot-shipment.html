Built to a practical Australian rural specification

The 7/90/30 designation describes a fence with seven horizontal line wires, an overall height of 900 mm, and 300 mm stay-wire spacing. It is a versatile configuration for rural boundaries, paddock division and livestock management.

Each roll in this shipment uses high-tensile wire to maintain fence tension over long runs. The fixed knot locks every line-and-stay intersection, helping the mesh keep its geometry under stock pressure and across changing terrain.

Stable fixed-knot geometry

Independent knots lock the line and stay wires together to resist movement and distortion.

High-tensile performance

Designed for strong, efficiently tensioned fence runs with reliable shape retention.

Verified zinc protection

W10 zinc coating manufactured to AS/NZS 4534 for dependable rural corrosion protection.
