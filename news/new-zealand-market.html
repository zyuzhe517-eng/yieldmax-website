YieldMax expands into the
New Zealand rural fencing market.

After more than a decade supplying Australia's rural fencing market, we are now partnering with New Zealand distributors and rural retailers — bringing fixed knot fence engineered for NZ's coastal, dairy and high-country conditions.

Ten years in Australia — now building in New Zealand

For over 10 years, YieldMax has supplied fixed knot fence to Australian rural fencing contractors, distributors and farm-supply retailers across every state. That decade has gone into refining one thing: consistent, dependable product on every order, backed by full documentation.

New Zealand farmers face their own mix of challenges — coastal salt, dairy-shed ammonia, deer and high-stock pressure, and steep high-country terrain. We have built our New Zealand offer around exactly those conditions, and we are now looking for the right distribution and retail partners to bring it to market.

Engineered for New Zealand conditions

🧂

Coastal & dairy corrosion

Our main NZ build uses a Zn-Al (Type ZA) coating to AS/NZS 4534 — roughly 2–4× the service life of standard galvanised in salt and high-ammonia environments.

🦌

Deer & high-stock pressure

HT line + HT stay wire holds its shape on impact — a true step-up from line-and-stay netting for deer, beef and high-pressure boundary fencing.

📐

Built to your specs

Custom heights, wire counts and stay spacings — including specifications that match common NZ Y-post and T-iron hole patterns.

What we offer distribution partners
